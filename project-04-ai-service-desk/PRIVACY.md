# Privacy Policy - CoolAir Demo

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

**CoolAir Demo** is a portfolio demonstration of an AI service desk built with n8n. It is not a commercial service. CoolAir is a fictional company.

## What data the app processes

The app only processes messages that are sent on purpose to the demo channels: the demo WhatsApp test number and the demo support email address.

For each message, the app may process:

- the sender's name, phone number or email address;
- the message text and the time it was received;
- details written in the message, such as city, address or the requested service.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to show how the demo works:

1. classify the request and draft a reply with an AI model (Google Gemini);
2. save the request in a private Google Sheet used as the service desk;
3. notify the demo team in a private Slack channel.

No reply is sent automatically. A person reviews every draft before it is sent.

## Sharing

The data is not sold or shared for advertising. It is processed only by the services the demo runs on: Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API), Google (Gmail, Google Sheets and Gemini) and Slack.

## Retention and deletion

Demo data is test data and is deleted regularly. To ask for your data to be deleted, open an issue in this repository or contact the author through the GitHub profile [JhontanC](https://github.com/JhontanC). Your data will be removed within 30 days.
